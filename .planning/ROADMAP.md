| Phase | Outcome | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Confirm cost/access/toolchain and falsifiable economic opportunity | G0: honest feasible path within zero spend |

Phase 3 economics begins in phase 0; schema-dependent optimizer/UI work can overlap phase 2 after the phase 1 contract freezes. Final integration waits for dependencies. The detailed task ledger is TASKS.md; authoritative gates and invariant contracts live in the master execution plan.
